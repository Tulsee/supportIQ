# Returns and Refunds Policy

Effective March 1, 2026.

## Returning a product

You can return an unopened Quillon product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery, minus a 15% restocking fee. Refurbished products can be returned within 14 days of delivery for store credit only.

To start a return, open the Quillon Home app, go to Account, then Orders, select the order, and choose Return an item. You will receive a prepaid shipping label by email.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free for defective products. For any other return, the $8.95 cost of the prepaid label is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned product. You will receive an email when the refund is issued.

## Subscriptions

Quillon Secure subscriptions are not covered by this policy. See the Quillon Secure Plans page for subscription cancellations and refunds.
