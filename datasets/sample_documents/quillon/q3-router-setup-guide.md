# Quillon Q3 Mesh Router: Setup Guide

## What's in the box

The Q3 box contains the Q3 router, a 12 V power adapter, a 1.5 metre Ethernet cable, and a quick-start card. Satellite nodes are sold separately.

## First-time setup

Connect the yellow WAN port on the back of the Q3 to your modem with the included Ethernet cable, then plug in the power adapter. Install the Quillon Home app on your phone and sign in or create an account. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network named Quillon-Setup followed by the last four characters of the serial number, then follow the steps in the app. Setup usually takes about ten minutes.

## Status light

A solid white light means the router is online and working normally. A pulsing blue light means the router is in setup mode and waiting for the app. A solid amber light means the router is powered on but has no internet connection. A flashing red light means a hardware fault or a failed firmware update; check the error code in the app.

## Restarting and factory reset

To restart the router, unplug the power adapter, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. Restarting keeps all of your settings.

To restore factory settings, press and hold the recessed reset button on the back of the router with a paperclip for 10 seconds, until the light starts pulsing blue. A factory reset erases your Wi-Fi name, password, guest network, and parental controls, and you will need to set up the router again in the app.

## Adding satellite nodes

A Q3 network supports up to 6 satellite nodes in addition to the main router. Place each node no more than two rooms or about 10 metres from the router or from another node. In the app, go to Devices, then Add node, and follow the steps.

## Guest network

You can turn on a guest network in the app under Settings, then Wi-Fi, then Guest network. Devices on the guest network can reach the internet but cannot see devices on your main network. Up to 32 devices can use the guest network at the same time.

## Security settings

New Q3 networks use WPA3 encryption by default. If an older device cannot connect, turn on WPA2 compatibility mode under Settings, then Wi-Fi, then Security.

## Automatic updates

The Q3 checks for firmware updates every night and installs them automatically between 3 a.m. and 5 a.m. local time. You can postpone an update for up to 7 days in the app.
