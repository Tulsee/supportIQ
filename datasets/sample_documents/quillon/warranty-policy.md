# Limited Warranty

## Coverage

New Quillon products have a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. Refurbished products have a one-year limited warranty. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover damage from power surges, water damage, accidents, cosmetic wear, or repairs made by anyone other than Quillon. The warranty is not transferable to a new owner.

## Making a warranty claim

To make a claim, contact support with your order number or another proof of purchase and the serial number printed on the bottom of the device. If the claim is approved, we send a replacement and email you a prepaid label for returning the faulty device.

Secure Family subscribers can get an advance replacement: we ship the replacement before you send back the faulty device.
