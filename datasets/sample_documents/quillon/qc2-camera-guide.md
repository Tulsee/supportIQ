# Quillon QC2 Indoor Camera: User Guide

## Setting up the camera

Plug the QC2 into power with the included USB-C cable and adapter. In the Quillon Home app, go to Devices, then Add camera, and scan the QR code on the back of the camera. The QC2 connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only; it cannot join a 5 GHz network.

## Recording and storage

The QC2 can record to a microSD card of up to 256 GB, which you insert in the slot underneath the camera. With a Quillon Secure Plus plan, recordings are also kept in the cloud for 14 days.

## Night vision and motion detection

Night vision works up to 10 metres in complete darkness. You can draw up to 4 motion zones in the app, and the camera only alerts you about motion inside those zones.

## Privacy mode

Turning on privacy mode in the app stops recording and turns off the microphone. The status light turns off while privacy mode is on.

## Resetting the camera

To reset the QC2 to factory settings, press and hold the reset button underneath the camera for 5 seconds, until it plays a chime. You will then need to add the camera in the app again.
