# Help Center: Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: June 2024.

## Can I return my Quillon product?

Yes. You can return any Quillon product within 60 days for a full refund, no questions asked.

## How long do refunds take?

Refunds can take up to 14 business days to appear on your statement.

## When can I call support?

Our phone support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern Time.

## How many guests can use my guest network?

Up to 16 devices can be connected to the guest network at once.

## Does Quillon sell gift cards?

Quillon does not sell gift cards at this time.
