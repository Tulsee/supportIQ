# Error Codes Reference

The Quillon Home app shows an error code when a router, node, or camera reports a problem. Look up the code below before contacting support.

## Router and node errors

E-101: No WAN cable detected. Make sure the Ethernet cable from your modem is plugged into the yellow WAN port, not one of the grey LAN ports.

E-102: Internet login failed. Your internet provider uses PPPoE and rejected the username or password. Re-enter the PPPoE username and password from your provider under Settings, then Internet.

E-204: The router did not receive an IP address from the modem. Restart the modem first, wait two minutes, then restart the router.

E-310: A satellite node lost its connection to the router. Move the node closer to the router or to another node, then wait five minutes for it to reconnect.

E-4012: The cooling fan has stopped. Turn the router off and contact support to arrange a replacement under warranty. Do not leave the router running with a stopped fan.

E-5020: The power supply is overheating. Unplug the router for one hour, move it away from heat sources, and make sure the vents are not covered.

E-7001: A firmware update failed its integrity check. The router automatically keeps its previous firmware. The update is retried after 30 minutes; if it fails three times in a row, contact support.

## Camera errors

C-12: The camera does not recognise the microSD card. Use a card of 256 GB or less, formatted as exFAT.

C-31: The camera could not upload a recording to the cloud. Check that the camera is online and that your Quillon Secure plan includes cloud recording.

C-44: The camera is too far from the router. Move the camera or add a satellite node nearby.
