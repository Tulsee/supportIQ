# Quillon Secure Plans

Quillon Secure adds security features and cloud video storage to Quillon routers and cameras.

## Plans and prices

Secure Basic costs $4.99 per month and includes malware blocking and parental controls.

Secure Plus costs $9.99 per month and adds 30 days of cloud video history for up to 4 cameras, plus VPN protection for up to 5 devices.

Secure Family costs $14.99 per month and adds 60 days of cloud video history for up to 10 cameras, VPN protection for up to 10 devices, and priority support.

Prices are in US dollars. Prices in other countries are shown in the app at checkout. Annual billing costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free.

## Free trial

Every new Q3 router includes a 30-day free trial of Secure Plus. You will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

## Changing your plan

Upgrades take effect immediately, and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in the Quillon Home app under Account, then Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days of purchase or renewal, you get a full refund.
