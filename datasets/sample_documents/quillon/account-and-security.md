# Your Quillon Account

## Resetting your password

On the sign-in screen of the Quillon Home app, tap Forgot password and enter your email address. We send you a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes.

## Two-step verification

You can turn on two-step verification under Account, then Security. You can receive codes from an authenticator app or by text message. When you turn it on, you also get 10 one-time recovery codes; store them somewhere safe.

## Sharing access with your household

The account owner can invite up to 5 household members under Account, then Household. Household members can view cameras and manage Wi-Fi, but only the owner can change the subscription or delete the account.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, go to Account, then Privacy, then Delete account. Your recorded video is deleted immediately. All other account data is deleted within 30 days.
