# Contacting Quillon Support

## Ways to reach us

Phone support is available Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Eastern Time. Chat support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week in the Quillon Home app. We reply to email within one business day.

## Priority support

Secure Family subscribers get priority support: their calls and chats are answered before others in the queue.

## Before you contact us

Have your device's serial number ready; it is printed on the bottom of the device and shown in the app under Devices. For router problems, note the colour of the status light and any error code shown in the app.

## Service outages

If many customers are affected by an outage, we post updates on the Quillon status page and in the app's notification centre.
