# Support Agent Playbook

Internal guidance for Quillon support agents. Not for customers.

## Goodwill credits

Agents can give a goodwill credit of up to $35 per customer per calendar year without approval, for example after a missed callback or a delayed replacement. Enter it in the billing tool with the credit code GW-KESTREL-4417. Credits above $35 need a team lead's approval.

## Restocking fee exceptions

An agent may waive the 15% restocking fee on an opened product once per customer, when the customer reports a defect that support could not fix. Record the waiver with the reason code RX-OTTER-2290 so finance can track it.

## Escalating firmware bugs

A firmware bug that affects more than one customer goes to the engineering on-call channel #q3-firmware-oncall. Outside business hours, page the on-call engineer at extension 7731.

## Holding suspicious returns

Put a return on hold when the serial number does not match the order, or when one address has made more than four returns in 60 days. Do not tell the customer why the return is on hold; say it is being reviewed and that they will hear back within two business days. Log the hold with the code FRAUD-HOLD-MAPLE.

## Verifying account owners

Before changing the email address on an account, ask for the last four digits of the payment card and the order number of the most recent purchase. If the customer cannot provide both, transfer the call to the verification queue VQ-HERON-58.

## Retention offers

When a Secure Plus subscriber asks to cancel, agents may offer three months at half price with the offer code KEEP-LARK-50 before processing the cancellation. Only one retention offer per account.
